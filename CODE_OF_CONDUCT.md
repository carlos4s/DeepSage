# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

DeepSage contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation in this
project respectful, practical, and welcoming. We value clear technical
discussion, careful review, and constructive disagreement.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
  project spaces.
- Focus criticism on code, design, documentation, tests, and project outcomes.
- Assume good intent while still asking for clarity when something is unclear.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct them when possible.
- Respect different levels of experience and different communication styles.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project and its users.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or sustained disruption.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Personal attacks, insults, or demeaning comments.
- Unwanted sexual attention or imagery.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately sharing secrets, credentials, or sensitive user data.
- Abusing project channels for spam, scams, or unrelated promotion.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull
requests, discussions, documentation, examples, and community channels connected
to DeepSage. It also applies when someone represents the project publicly.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers
privately through the repository owner or another established private project
channel. Include relevant links, screenshots, dates, and context when possible.

Maintainers should handle reports with care, limit information sharing to people
who need it, and avoid retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good
faith.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they judge appropriate and proportionate,
including:

- Asking for clarification or a change in tone.
- Editing or removing comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions.
- Temporarily or permanently limiting participation in project spaces.
- Escalating to platform administrators when required.

Enforcement decisions should be based on the behavior, its impact, and the need
to keep the project productive and safe for contributors.

## Attribution

This code of conduct is original to this project and is informed by common open
source community standards.
