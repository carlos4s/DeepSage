# Small Modular Reactor Deployment Across North America and Europe (2026)

## Background

Small modular reactors (SMRs) are fission reactors with electrical output
typically under 300 MWe, designed for factory fabrication and modular site
assembly. Through 2025 most activity was concentrated in regulatory review and
first-of-a-kind construction; 2026 marks the first wave of post-licensing
deployment announcements in several Western markets.

## Project Status by Region

In the United States, the NRC's approval of NuScale's VOYGR-6 design in 2023
opened the door to a UAMPS-led 462 MWe project at Idaho National Laboratory,
though cost escalation led UAMPS to pause the project in early 2024 [1]. As of
2026, X-energy and Dow have moved forward with a Xe-100 high-temperature
gas-cooled reactor at Dow's Seadrift, Texas chemical site, with site
preparation underway [2].

In Canada, Ontario Power Generation's Darlington New Nuclear Project remains
the most advanced SMR build in the West, with four GE Hitachi BWRX-300 units
planned; first unit construction began in 2025 with target commercial
operation in 2029 [3].

Across Europe, the UK's Great British Nuclear selected six SMR designs for
its 2024 competition, narrowing to Rolls-Royce SMR, GE Hitachi, Westinghouse,
and Holtec in 2026 [4]. Poland's Orlen-Synthos Green Energy joint venture
continues siting work for six BWRX-300 units, while EDF and Czech state
utility ČEZ confirmed plans for Westinghouse AP300 deployments by 2032 [5].

## Outstanding Headwinds

Financing remains the dominant obstacle: the per-MWh cost of first-of-a-kind
SMRs in 2025–2026 contracts is running 30–50% above earlier estimates [1][2].
Supply-chain bottlenecks in pressure-vessel forgings and fuel availability
(particularly HALEU for advanced designs) constrain near-term schedules [6].
